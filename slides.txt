INTRODUCTION
What is OpsIQ?

OpsIQ is an AI-powered SRE Dashboard that monitors your entire AWS 
infrastructure in real time, detects anomalies before they become outages, 
and gives your engineers instant AI-driven root cause analysis and 
remediation steps.

01  See Everything
    Real-time health scores across all AWS accounts on one screen

02  Understand Instantly
    AI root cause analysis in seconds — not hours of log archaeology

03  Act with Confidence
    CLI commands, investigation steps, and runbook links on demand

A single pane of glass for your AWS estate — the AI intelligence layer 
above your existing monitoring.

----
GOALS OF THE INITIATIVE
Why We Built This

1  Centralise visibility
   Give CloudOps teams a single screen covering all AWS accounts — no more 
   jumping between consoles or piecing together alerts from multiple tools.

2  Reduce mean time to understand (MTTU)
   Cut the time between 'alert fired' and 'we know what's wrong' from 45+ 
   minutes to under 2 minutes using AI-generated root cause analysis.

3  Put AI in the operator's hands — on demand
   Make Amazon Bedrock AI available to every on-call engineer as a tool they 
   control, not an automated system that runs loose and generates surprise costs.

4  Validate the architecture for production
   Prove the end-to-end pipeline — signal ingestion, anomaly detection, incident 
   correlation, AI summarisation — works reliably before connecting real account data.

KEY CAPABILITIES
What You'll See in the Demo

1  Fleet Health Dashboard                              [Zero Bedrock]
   13 accounts monitored simultaneously. Health score 0–100 per account, 
   trend arrows showing direction, avg fleet health, and real-time alert 
   banner for critical accounts.

2  AI Incident Analysis                    [1 Bedrock call · cached forever]
   Click any HIGH severity incident → Generate Summary → Amazon Bedrock 
   returns root cause, investigation steps, CLI commands, confidence level, 
   and resolution estimate in seconds.

3  AI SRE Agent — Chat                        [1 Bedrock call per message]
   Dedicated investigation workspace. Ask anything about your fleet in plain 
   English. Mode tabs: Investigate, Summarize, Runbook, Query Logs. 
   CLI commands copy with one click.

4  Infrastructure Topology                             [Zero Bedrock]
   Dependency graph per account — swim lanes showing Ingress → Compute → 
   Data → Storage. AWS service icons, hover tooltips, zoom and pan. 
   Built from live topology data.
