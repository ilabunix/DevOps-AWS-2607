Incident Number


Issue Recap
Customers accessing the EUAC Center through FedLine Advantage (FLA) are receiving 503/5xx errors. EUAC remains accessible through FedLine Web (FLW).

Services / Applications Impacted
FedLine – EUAC Center access via FLA.

Business / Customer Impact
Customers attempting to access EUAC Center through FLA are impacted. Customers can continue using FLW for the same functions. Other FLA applications remain available.

Current Findings
The technical bridge identified a configuration mismatch specific to the FLA MyFedLine/EUAC junction. The affected junction is directing traffic toward the Colo production path rather than the intended On-Prem production backend.

Application JVM and database checks are healthy. The corrective action is understood and would involve updating the affected junction configuration, followed by a rolling restart of the WebSEAL containers.

Current Decision / Status
Business stakeholders have decided to leave the application in its current state and defer the corrective change until the regular weekend maintenance window.

The team will continue monitoring customer impact. If customer impact increases materially before the weekend, Business and technical resources will regroup and reassess whether the change needs to be performed earlier.

Next Steps
Technical teams will complete comparison/validation of the affected junction against other known-good junction configurations and confirm that no other planned weekend activity conflicts with the corrective change. The junction routing update and rolling restart are planned for the weekend maintenance window, followed by validation of EUAC access through FLA.
