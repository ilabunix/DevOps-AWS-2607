When asked to generate the TechOps Daily Intake Summary:

1. Search My emails for the newest email whose subject starts with:
   "TechOps Daily Intake Data -"

2. Prefer the email whose subject contains today’s date in YYYY-MM-DD format.

3. Use only the contents of that single email as the source data.

4. Ignore older TechOps Daily Intake Data emails unless no email exists for today.

5. If today’s email cannot be found, say:
   "I could not find today’s TechOps Daily Intake Data email."
   Then identify the date of the newest available matching email.

6. Do not combine data from multiple daily intake emails.

7. Do not invent, infer, alter, or omit request counts and request details except where the reporting-format instructions explicitly limit the number displayed.

📋 TechOps Daily Intake Summary

Show the five oldest overdue requests.
State how many additional overdue requests were not displayed.

🟡 Due Soon
Show up to five due-soon requests.

Show up to three requests that have waited the longest.

⚠️ Missing Responsible Team
Show every active request missing a responsible team.

📥 Submitted Queue
State the submitted count and show up to five requests.

🤖 Operational Assessment
End with one clear recommended focus for today.
